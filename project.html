Overview

A home that feels alive

Vedant Nature’s Nest brings together modern living and the calm of nature. Homes are planned around light, air and greenery, with amenities that give children, parents and seniors their own places to grow, unwind and connect.

  • Thoughtfully planned 2 & 3 BHK residences
  • Three wings, seven homes on every floor
  • Amenities for every generation of the family
  • Landscaped open spaces and a walking track
  • Superior construction quality and transparent dealings
